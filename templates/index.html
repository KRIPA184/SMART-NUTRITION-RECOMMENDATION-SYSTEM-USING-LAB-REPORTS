How It Works

Phase 1: Data Ingestion

Upload your lab report (PDF or image). Our OCR engine extracts biomarkers like Hemoglobin, Glucose, HbA1c, Cholesterol, Vitamins, and more with regex-based precision.

Phase 2: AI/ML Analysis

RandomForest classifies your health risk. LightGBM predicts personalized nutrient targets. SHAP explains which biomarkers influence your results most.

Phase 3: Smart Recommendations

Clinical rule engine filters critical values. Google Gemini AI generates a personalized diet plan, weekly meal plan, and lifestyle advice — all from validated data only.
